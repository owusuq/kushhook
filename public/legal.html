Terms of Use

Kushhook is a small chat and community app. By signing in or creating a profile you agree to these terms.

Who can use it

  • You must be 18 or over.
  • You are responsible for your account and for your chat passphrase. Nobody can recover the passphrase for you.

How to behave

  • Be respectful. No harassment, hate, threats, scams, spam or impersonation.
  • Do not share illegal content or anything that sexualises or harms children.
  • Do not post other people's private information without their permission.
  • Do not try to break, overload or get around the app's security.
  • Only share links and content you are allowed to share.

Reports and enforcement

  • You can report a person, message or post. The admin reviews reports.
  • The admin can delete posts and messages, suspend an account, or remove an account for good if these terms are broken.

Your content

You keep ownership of what you write and share. You give Kushhook permission to store and show it so the app can work. Feed posts can be seen by other members.

No guarantees

Kushhook is provided as it is, without promises that it will always be available or error free. We are not responsible for what members post, or for losses caused by lost passphrases, lost devices or outages, as far as the law allows.

Changes and leaving

These terms may change. If they do, the date above is updated. You can stop using Kushhook and delete your account at any time from your profile.

Privacy Policy

What we collect

  • Sign-in: when you use Google sign-in, we receive your Google name, email address and account ID. This is handled by Google and Firebase Authentication.
  • Profile: the name, username, colour, avatar and short bio you choose, and a confirmation that you are 18 or over.
  • Activity: friend requests and connections, when you were last online, and a "typing" signal while you type in a chat.
  • Chats: your messages and files are encrypted on your device before they are stored, so only you and the person you are chatting with can read them. Kushhook and its admin cannot read them. Photos, videos and files are temporary and are removed when the chat is closed.
  • Feed posts: text you post to the feed. Members can see these.
  • Reports: if you report someone, the report goes to the admin with your reason. Reporting a message also sends that message's text and the sender's username to the admin.

What we use it for

Only to run Kushhook: signing you in, showing your profile, delivering chats, and keeping the community safe. We do not sell your data. We do not show ads. We do not use analytics or advertising trackers.

Who handles it

Kushhook is built on Google Firebase (sign-in, database and hosting), so your data is stored on Google's servers. The app also loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google can see your IP address when the page loads. The admin can see members' names, usernames and reports, but not encrypted chat contents.

Your choices

  • Edit your username, bio and avatar any time in your profile.
  • Clear a chat or delete your own messages and posts.
  • Delete your account from your profile. This removes your messages, posts, encryption keys and profile, and then your sign-in.
  • Questions or requests about your data: tell the admin using the Report button, or contact the person who invited you.

Keeping data safe

Chats are end-to-end encrypted and access to data is limited by security rules. No online service is perfectly secure, so do not share anything you would not want to risk losing. If you forget your chat passphrase, old messages cannot be recovered.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page is updated.

Cookies and storage

Kushhook uses only what it needs to work on your device. It does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

  • Staying signed in: Firebase Authentication keeps a sign-in session in your browser's storage.
  • Your chat key: a locked copy of your encryption key is kept on your device so you do not have to type your passphrase every time.
  • Small settings: which chats you have read, whether you dismissed the app download bar, and whether you dismissed the notice about storage.

You can clear all of this any time in your browser or phone settings. You will then need to sign in again and unlock your chats with your passphrase.
